32M

The extension of AMO coverage to 32 million beneficiaries is intensifying the cash-flow crisis faced by private clinics — driven by the long payment delays of institutional payers.

AMO (Assurance Maladie Obligatoire) is Morocco's national compulsory health-insurance system, covering salaried employees, the self-employed and eligible citizens through public and private schemes.
